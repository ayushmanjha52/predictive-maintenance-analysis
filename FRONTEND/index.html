ALERTS & FINDINGS

Curated from the full 7-month dataset — not auto-generated noise, each one is traceable to specific events below.

April spike: shared root cause, not 5 independent failures

A single-day cluster of ~10 "Saw#2 feedback missing" proximity events plus recurring furnace cycle-gap photocell issues explain most of April's jump. Treat as 2 targeted maintenance items, not a general reliability decline.

Encoder — highest severity per incident

Averages 41.4 min per event (vs. 22.8 for Photocell) and carries the highest FMEA RPN (240) of any device with a completed FMEA sheet. Best evidence-based case for prioritized redesign or Air Purging investment.

LVDT — second-highest severity + high RPN

RPN 216 (avg 168.5) combined with 36.9 min avg per incident. FMEA risk and real-world outcome agree — a rare and credible convergence for a report.

Photocell — volume leader, not severity leader

102 events (most of any device) but only 22.8 min average — moderate severity, high frequency. Priority action here is process discipline (cleaning cadence, air purge reliability), not redesign.

Data coverage gap

47.2% of the 655 total delay events could not be resolved to a specific field device — either genuinely non-field-device delays (furnace, motor, crane) or reason text too ambiguous. Reported as-is, not rounded up.

FMEA gap: Photocell & Proximity

These are your #1 and #5 empirical contributors but have no FMEA sheet in the data provided. Closing this gap would let the priority ranking be fully evidence-based across all five devices.
